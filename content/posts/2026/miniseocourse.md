I am no expert in SEO, Search Engine Optimization, but here are a few things I have done with this site. 

## Registration, Cleanup, and Technical Basics

- Register the site in [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console) and [Bing Webmaster Tools](https://www.bing.com/webmasters),
   and fix issues that they report.
- Add clear **meta descriptions** to every post so search results show a useful summary instead of random text snippets.
- Clean up internal and external links so there are no broken paths or inconsistent URLs.
- Make sure `sitemap.xml` and `robots.txt` are accurate, and submitted to the search consoles.

## Measure

- Add basic web analytics (I wrote about my setup in [How and why I created www.arvesv.com](/posts/2026/thissite/)).
- Combine analytics with search console reports to see which queries bring visitors in and what people actually click on.

Doing this told me that you, i.e. my (limited) audience, is clearly interested in security-related topics.
